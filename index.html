Data source, sample rules, and definitions
  • Source: Voteview Hall_members.csv, embedded in this HTML. All chart data and scripts are local; no network request is needed.
  • Rows and years: House records from the 96th through the latest available 119th Congress. A freshman is a member’s first recorded House appearance. People are identified by bioguide ID where present, with ICPSR ID as fallback.
  • Matching: Each freshman is matched to the immediately preceding Congress’s member with the same state abbreviation and normalized district code. Keep only seats with exactly one current occupant and exactly one prior occupant. Exclude explicit special-election arrivals (last_means = 2); duplicate occupants prevent a potentially ambiguous mid-term seat match. Blank election-method codes remain eligible when the seat match is unique.
  • Redistricting exclusions: Exclude incoming freshman rows in the 98th, 103rd, 108th, 113th, and 118th Congresses because district numbers alone do not guarantee geographic seat continuity after redistricting. Vertical dashed markers identify those Congresses.
  • Extremity: For either selected metric, extremity is abs(score). Net shift is the paired freshman extremity minus predecessor extremity. The “largest upward extremity jump” is the largest within-Congress paired net shift; if it is negative, no matched freshman in that Congress was more extreme than their predecessor.
  • Party categories: Party codes 100 (Democrat), 200 (Republican), and 328 (Independent) are kept as recorded. Democrat/Republican filters use the incoming freshman’s party. Same-party/party-flip compare the two members’ party codes in their respective Congresses.
  • Region categories: Freshman state is assigned to the four Census-style regions: Northeast (CT, ME, MA, NH, RI, VT, NJ, NY, PA); Midwest (IL, IN, MI, OH, WI, IA, KS, MN, MO, NE, ND, SD); South (DE, FL, GA, MD, NC, SC, VA, DC, WV, AL, KY, MS, TN, AR, LA, OK, TX); West (AZ, CO, ID, MT, NV, NM, UT, WY, AK, CA, HI, OR, WA). DC is South. Records outside these regions remain in All regions and are omitted for a named region selection.
